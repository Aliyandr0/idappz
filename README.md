# IDAPPZ

A mobile app (React Native / Expo, iOS and Android) that brings together personal discipline tools, end-to-end encrypted messaging, and an on-device "digital clone" companion.

Website: https://www.idappz.com

## What it does

Digital discipline: daily task planning, focus and detox sessions, streaks, and honest progress statistics built from real phone data.

Digital clone: a companion that runs on the device, learns your routine, helps plan your day in seven languages, and keeps its memory private.

Private messaging: end-to-end encrypted chats, groups and a social feed.

Clone world: a 3D room (Unity) where the clone lives, with care actions, bond levels and mini-games.

## How we use Claude

Developing and reviewing the codebase with Claude Code. Evaluating and improving the clone's conversation quality (multilingual planning, safety replies, natural dialogue). Server-side AI orchestration for the clone.
